## 5. RAL disclosure gate

The `/ral` page can unlock gross salary figures after email verification:

1. You enter a work email (disposable / temporary addresses are rejected).
2. A one-time code is emailed to you.
3. After verification, a time-limited session lets the client fetch amounts from the gate API.
4. When the session expires, amounts stop being served and the UI clears them.

Email and verification logs are retained only as needed to operate the gate, prevent abuse, and handle support — not for marketing. Do not use a personal address you are unwilling to associate with this request.
