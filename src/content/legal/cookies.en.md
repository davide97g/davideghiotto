This cookie policy describes how [davideghiotto.it](https://davideghiotto.it) uses cookies and similar technologies (including `localStorage`). It complements the [Privacy policy](/privacy).

The Site is aimed at visitors in Italy and the European Union. Non-essential analytics technologies run **only after consent**.

## 1. What are cookies and similar technologies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Similar technologies (such as `localStorage`) can store preferences or session tokens in the browser without using an HTTP cookie.

## 2. How I ask for consent

On your first visit (or after you clear site data), a banner lets you:

- **Accept all** — necessary + analytics
- **Reject all** — necessary only; analytics stays off
- **Customize** — turn analytics on or off, then save

You can reopen preferences anytime via **Manage cookies** in the footer.

Until you accept analytics, Google Analytics storage remains denied (Consent Mode defaults) and analytics events are not sent from this Site.

## 3. Categories

### Necessary (always active)

Required for basic operation of a service you request (language, RAL unlock session). These do not require consent under ePrivacy rules for strictly necessary storage.

### Analytics (optional — consent)

Google Analytics 4 measures visits and interactions so I can understand how the Site is used. No advertising cookies are set by this Site for Google Ads personalization.

## 4. Table of technologies

| Name | Type | Category | Provider | Duration | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `lang` | `localStorage` | Necessary | This Site | Until cleared | Remember EN/IT language choice |
| `dg-consent-v1` | `localStorage` | Necessary | This Site | Until cleared / updated | Store your cookie preferences |
| `dg-ral-access-v2` | `localStorage` | Necessary | This Site | Until session expiry / cleared | Remember RAL unlock metadata (no amounts) |
| `dg-ral-token-v2` | `localStorage` | Necessary | This Site | Until session expiry / cleared | Short-lived token to fetch unlocked RAL data |
| `_ga` | Cookie | Analytics | Google Analytics | Up to 2 years (Google default) | Distinguish visitors |
| `_ga_*` | Cookie | Analytics | Google Analytics | Up to 2 years (Google default) | Persist GA4 session / campaign state |

Exact Google cookie lifetimes can vary with Google’s product defaults. Analytics cookies appear only after you grant analytics consent.

## 5. Google Analytics 4

When analytics is accepted, the Site loads `gtag.js` from Google and configures measurement ID `G-BTP8Z49CM7` with IP anonymization where supported and manual page views for this single-page app.

Google may process data outside the EEA. See the [Privacy policy](/privacy) and [Google’s privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 6. Managing and deleting

- Use **Manage cookies** on this Site to change or withdraw analytics consent.
- You can also delete cookies and site data from your browser settings.
- Browser “Do Not Track” signals are not used as a substitute for the consent banner on this Site.

Rejecting analytics does not reduce access to the public content of the Site. The RAL gate still requires email verification when you choose to unlock figures — that is separate from analytics cookies.
